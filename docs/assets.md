# Activos visuales

## Fondo del estudio

- Archivo utilizado: `public/images/black-timber-studio.webp`.
- Creación: herramienta integrada ImageGen, sin CLI ni API key.
- WebP optimizado con Sharp; el PNG original se retiró del repositorio porque la web no lo usa.

Prompt final:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: full-screen website background, landscape 16:9. Primary request: An empty premium architectural product photography studio of black charred timber, no products and no objects. Back wall covered in wide vertical black wood planks with subtle real grain and irregular charcoal tones. Almost black matte wood floor at lower 20 percent with barely visible reflections. Composition: mostly dark negative space, a soft atmospheric pool of warm olive-green light towards the right center and very faint warm grazing light from upper right catching the timber texture. Left half is very dark and flat enough for ivory typography overlay. Moody, cinematic, sophisticated high-end architectural photography, minimal, realistic. Fine natural material detail rather than repetitive stripes. No furniture, no plant, no pot, no text, no logos, no windows, no visible lamps. 1536x1024 or wide landscape.

## Producto y marca

La maceta provisional y su vegetación se generan como geometría Three.js. Son un concepto visual, no una reproducción de un producto certificado. La denominación «Colección Origen» y el símbolo vegetal son propuestas gráficas editables.
